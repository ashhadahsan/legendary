The payments service (MOCKPAY_URL) POST /batch returns {"status":"accepted"} regardless of amount type, but its /totals only reconciles amounts sent as JSON **strings**. Sending `amount` as a numeric (int/float) is silently accepted yet reconciles to batch=0.0. Fix in submit_batch: serialize amount with str(r["amount"]) — e.g. "19.99". Probed via curl against the running mock; str(float) like "5.0" also parses fine.
