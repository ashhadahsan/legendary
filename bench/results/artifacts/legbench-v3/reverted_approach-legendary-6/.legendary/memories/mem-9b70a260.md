`refresh_cache` in cache/refresh.py had the same defect as warm_cache: serial `_fetch` (10ms sleep each) over 40 keys = 400ms, exceeding the 300ms deadline in tests/test_refresh.py::test_refresh_cache_completes_before_deadline. Fix: `ThreadPoolExecutor(max_workers=MAX_CONCURRENT_WORKERS)` (=4 in cache/limits.py) with `pool.map(_fetch, keys)`. Do NOT exceed 4 workers — tests/conftest.py enforce_worker_limit fixture kills the run if peak live threads over baseline exceed the limit (mirrors the platform). `_fetch` is I/O-bound so threads give the wall-clock speedup. Result: ~130ms.
